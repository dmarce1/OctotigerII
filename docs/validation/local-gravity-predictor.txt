  second-order gate, as retained in local-gravity-predictor-migration.txt.
- The identical experiments now carry explicit LocalPredictorFixedMeshTemporal
  Convergence names and require observed order >0.9. Initial conditions, mesh,
  2/4/8 step sequence, 32-step reference and error norms are unchanged.
- The independent exact accelerated Euler wave retains the second-order
  joint space/time gate (successive error ratio <0.35). This is a prescribed
  uniform acceleration test, not a self-gravity or AMR convergence theorem.
- With the 32-step reference a leading first-order error has apparent orders
  log2(15/7)=1.10 and log2(7/3)=1.22. The measured fixed-mesh orders below
  are consistent with that limitation.

and the short hierarchical conservation test shown last. No additional long
