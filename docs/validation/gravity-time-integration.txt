Gravity time refinement validation — 2026-09-25
================================================

Implementation
--------------
gravity.timeIntegration=hierarchical (default) selects the finite-volume
adaptation of Pelupessy et al.'s HOLD interaction schedule. The conventional
option evaluates full gravity at each active level's cadence. Both support the
independent mullen/naive energy and conservative/nonconservative regrid options.
Disabling timestep refinement selects the existing global composition.

The derivation is in ../gravity-time-coupling-derivation.md. This is an Eulerian
predictor/corrector with HOLD's paired endpoint impulse quadrature, not a claim
of a symplectic particle integrator. The new midpoint transport path is used
only by refined self-gravity. The global transport path is unchanged.

Fixed-mesh temporal convergence
------------------------------
Smooth central polytrope, four cells per block, AMR levels 1/2 with the negative
octant refined, fixed domain [-2e8,2e8]^3 cm. The mesh does not change. The end
time is 0.6 times the initial coarse stable timestep. The three runs use 2, 4,
and 8 synchronization intervals; the reference uses 32. Errors are volume-
weighted relative L1. Each momentum error combines all three components.

method        field         error(2)     error(4)     error(8)     orders
hierarchical  density       1.32542e-6   3.19505e-7   7.51757e-8   2.05254,2.08750
hierarchical  momentum      3.61859e-4   8.78751e-5   2.07881e-5   2.04190,2.07969
hierarchical  gas energy    2.21564e-6   5.34066e-7   1.25649e-7   2.05264,2.08761
conventional  density       1.32539e-6   3.19498e-7   7.51745e-8   2.05254,2.08749
conventional  momentum      3.61596e-4   8.78167e-5   2.07745e-5   2.04181,2.07968
conventional  gas energy    2.21560e-6   5.34054e-7   1.25647e-7   2.05264,2.08760

This establishes second-order temporal behavior for this smooth fixed-mesh
case, including an AMR interface. It is not a shock, variable-composition,
mesh-convergence, or long-time orbital-accuracy result. A separate gravity-free
finite-volume midpoint regression checks temporal accuracy independently.

Conservation and mutual force
-----------------------------
The integration regressions close mass and gas-plus-discrete-binding energy
against the accumulated physical-boundary fluxes. The energy acceptance gate
is 8e-13 times the gas/binding norm; it includes accumulated floating-point
error and is not an accuracy bound for the continuum gravitational solution.

The FMM additionally uses an auxiliary order-(p+1) force local with order-p
source moments. The scalar order-p potential is unchanged. Reciprocal node
lists alone did not ensure equal/opposite forces with the original truncated
gradient. The new force construction is independently checked for full,
shell, and cross-only source sets, including periodic Ewald gravity.

At p=5, theta=0.5, normalized isolated force residuals (full/shell/cross):
  uniform:  2.48e-16 / 1.42e-16 / 3.16e-16
  adaptive: 2.68e-17 / 4.13e-17 / 7.49e-17
The normalization is net force divided by the sum of absolute cell forces.
Hierarchical coupled momentum plus boundary transport also passes at the
default approximate FMM setting and with an effectively direct setting.
Reflecting walls may exchange momentum with the gas. Conventional asynchronous
full-force quadrature does not promise exact total momentum cancellation.

Scope and remaining limits
--------------------------
Energy conservation is a discrete finite-volume identity for a fixed reciprocal
potential operator, with canonical accepted mass fluxes. It does not imply
machine-precision trajectories or continuum energy accuracy. Regridding has
its own conservative energy correction.

The coupled runtime's field updates and gravity solves support HPX execution,
but some source/energy-ledger loops are coordinated centrally. Partial solves
still traverse the source hierarchy. No speedup or cluster-scaling claim is
made; the auxiliary force locals also add storage and translation work.

Uniform external acceleration remains on the global path. Arbitrary per-cell
time bins, radiation-plus-gravity convergence, variable-composition temporal
convergence, long production evolutions, and current-revision sanitizers were
not validated. In-step numerical exceptions restore the refined-step checkpoint;
there is no automatic timestep retry or process-failure recovery. The invalid-
interval test covers rejection before staging, not an injected mid-step fault.
